# Common Cardiovascular Pathologies

This week we cover several disorders of the heart and vascular system.

## Rhythm and rate

The most common arrhythmia in our ED is **tachycardia**, often presenting alongside chest pain. **Bradycardia** may indicate sinus node dysfunction.

## Inflammation

**Endocarditis** (inflammation of the heart's inner lining) requires urgent antibiotic therapy. **Pericarditis** is similar but affects the surrounding sac.

## Ischemic and structural disease

**Myocardial infarction** is death of heart muscle tissue from coronary occlusion. **Atherosclerosis** — the buildup of fatty plaque in arteries — is the major risk factor. Long-standing **hypertension** often produces **cardiomegaly**. Right-sided heart failure can present with **hepatomegaly** and peripheral edema.

## Diagnostics and management

**Angiography** is the gold standard for visualizing coronary arteries. Patients with multiple risk factors are typically referred to a **cardiologist** for ongoing management. Common labs include screening for **hyperlipidemia** and **hyperglycemia**.
